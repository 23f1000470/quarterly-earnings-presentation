Financial Formulae

Present Value of Free Cash Flow (DCF):

\( \mathrm{PV} = \sum_{t=1}^{T} \dfrac{FCF_t}{(1+r)^t} \)

Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC):

\( \mathrm{WACC} = \dfrac{E}{E+D}r_e + \dfrac{D}{E+D}r_d(1-\tau) \)
